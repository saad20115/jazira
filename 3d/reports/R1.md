> **Paths:** since R3a the project lives at `C:\aljazira-3d` (the S: share is offline). Read every `S:\...` path below as `C:\...`.

Clay only: no materials, lighting or look-dev. Built in Blender 5.2.3 through the blender MCP tools, plus background Blender runs for the previews. No third-party generators, no downloaded assets.

- the 5 long camera animations are replaced by a static per-system rig (CAM/PIVOT/PATH/HOT) and quick hero → system → back previews;
- the city is cut to a ring of low blocks;
- all geometry is low-poly from the start.

I added the revision as section 6 of `ref\A-1-3d-assets.md`, as requested. Nothing else in `ref\` was touched.

## Collections and nodes
Collections are exactly `ARCH_facade, ARCH_core, ARCH_podium, SITE, CONTEXT, SYS_hvac, SYS_fire, SYS_plumbing, SYS_power, SYS_smart, CAMERAS, LIGHTS`. Each has a **root Empty with the same name**, and every object in it is parented to that root, so the GLB gets the nodes `ARCH_facade`, `SYS_hvac`, and so on.

- **Podium glazing is in ARCH_facade** (`FAC_podium_glass`), not ARCH_podium. It is curtain wall and must ghost, otherwise the GF/L01 plant can't be seen. ARCH_podium = plinth, podium roof/frame, canopies.
- `LIGHTS` is empty in R1 (Workbench studio light only).

## View rig (for Three.js)
**CAM_hero**: lens **63.28 mm**, 36 mm sensor, horizontal fit, square, so FOV = **31.75°** both ways. **shift_y −0.227**. Location (68.3, −252.5, 65.5), rotation XYZ (90°, 0°, 16.67°).
- It is a level camera with a shift lens, because the reference has parallel verticals (two-point perspective).
- In Three.js, reproduce the shift with `camera.setViewOffset(W, H, 0, 0.227*H, W, H)`. Please verify the sign on the first build. A plain `lookAt` without the offset will frame the tower too high.
- I solved the camera from 6 silhouette points and refined it against the edge overlay. The tower outline is within about 5 px. The reference is not perspective-consistent (its podium implies other vanishing points), so the podium corner is off by about 20 px.
- `tower-render.png` is the camera background image.

**PIVOT_hero** (CAMERAS) is the point on CAM_hero's optical axis nearest the tower axis, at (−6.7, −2.0, 65.5). It is the OrbitControls target for the first view. `lookAt(PIVOT_hero)` from CAM_hero gives exactly CAM_hero's rotation.

Per system, in collection `SYS_<s>` under its root (except the cameras):

| System | CAM_<s> (in CAMERAS) eye → looks at PIVOT_<s> | Lens | PATH_ waypoints | HOT_<s>_n (`label` custom property) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| hvac | (34, −30, 112) → (−1, 8, 83) | 30 | none | chillers, cooling_towers, ahus_L22, duct_riser, fcus_L12 |
| fire | (−60, 30, 14) → (−24, 10, 2.5) | 30 | `PATH_fire_1` (−97.0, −61.7, 48.4) | fire_pumps, jockey_pump, sprinkler_riser, floor_mains_L10, alarm_panel |
| plumbing | (−50, 40, 96) → (−15, 3, 80) | 28 | `PATH_plumbing_1` (−112.0, −58.0, 90.7) | roof_tanks, water_risers, drainage_stacks, break_tanks_B1, booster_pumps_B1 |
| power | (54, 34, 12) → (17, 9, 2) | 30 | none | generator, transformers, mv_switchgear, lv_panels, cable_risers |
| smart | (−50, −16, 11) → (−25, 9, 6.5) | 26 | none | bms_data_room, bms_console, elv_riser, cctv_entrance, access_control |

**How waypoints are chosen** (`build_views.py`): a straight hero → CAM move must keep an **8 m clearance tube** (8 m sideways and up, 4 m down; ground and plaza props ignored).
- A plain single-ray test passed every system, but fire, plumbing and smart visibly skimmed the façade in the previews. So I made the test stricter.
- Where a move fails, the script inserts the midpoint of an orbit around the sliding pivot. Only fire and plumbing needed one; they now swing out round the west side.

**Move used in the previews** (what the web should do):
- position eased (smootherstep) along hero → PATH… → CAM;
- look-at target eased from PIVOT_hero to PIVOT_<s>;
- lens and shift eased to the system camera (shift → 0);
- `facade_opacity` eased 1 → 0.15.

The way back is the same move in reverse.

**facade_opacity**: Blender collections cannot carry animated properties, so it is a custom property (0–1, default 1.0) on the **`ARCH_facade` root Empty**. It exports to the GLB as node extras. In Blender it drives each façade mesh's object-colour alpha, which is why the Workbench previews show the ghosting. In R2 the glass becomes glTF-PBR alpha-blend (no transmission), so the web can set opacity per frame.

## Questions for the CTO
1. **Basement plumbing.** The break tanks and boosters are in B1, as briefed. In the live model a visitor can't see underground unless the ground/plaza/GF slab also ghosts when the plumbing system is entered. Options:
   - (a) also fade the GF slab and plaza when plumbing is active;
   - (b) move the tanks and boosters to a GF rear plant room (my recommendation: clean and readable).
   
   Which do you want?
2. **One entry view per system.** For example, CAM_hvac shows the roof plant, while the L22 AHUs and the L06/12/18 FCUs are reached by orbiting around PIVOT_hvac. Is one entry view plus orbit enough, or do you want secondary pivots per system (for example PIVOT_hvac_L22)?
3. **Slabs while inside.** Should floor slabs (ARCH_core) also fade, to about 40–60%, when a system is entered? At the moment they chop the risers into segments.
4. **Hero podium mismatch.** The reference isn't geometrically consistent, so the podium corner is off by about 20 px while the tower outline matches within about 5 px. Is that acceptable?
5. **Backdrop.** For R2, do you prefer real-time fog plus the low blocks, or a blurred dusk plate (a sphere or backdrop card) plus fewer blocks? The plate looks closer to the reference and is cheaper in tris but costs about 0.5–1 MB of texture.
