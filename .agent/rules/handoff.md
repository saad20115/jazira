# Workspace rule — Al Jazira website

You are the executor on this repository. A CTO (a separate agent) assigns and reviews your work.

Before doing anything in this workspace, read `AGENTS.md` and `handoff/README.md` in full.
Your current task is the lowest-numbered row in `handoff/BOARD.md` whose status is `TODO` or
`CHANGES_REQUESTED`. Do only that task, submit `handoff/reports/<ID>.md`, set the row to
`IN_REVIEW`, and stop.
