step is a separate commit that passes `pnpm proof:compare` (R-2) with zero pixel, a11y-tree and DOM

## Scope
- **Structure (zero change):**
  - split `src/app/(frontend)/[locale]/page.tsx` into per-section server components (section 4 is
    already `systems3d/`, owned by P3-T5);
  - merge the two near-identical marquees;
  - one Arabic-gate helper; one metadata builder; one site-origin resolver;
  - a shared CMS getter factory (the 8 copied getters);
  - remove dead CSS, props and dictionary keys;
  - unify the breakpoints;
  - move route-file helpers to `src/lib`.
- **Behaviour fixes (each with a test):**
  - **C-02:** the TeamCard reveal is hidden only under `html[data-motion="on"]` (§20), and is visible
    without IntersectionObserver;
  - **C-05:** font preloads come from the build manifest at build time, not a per-request disk scan;
    there must be **no Arabic CLS regression** (R-3 of P3-T1);
  - **C-10:** the hero video gets a pause control, is static under reduced motion, and does not loop
    endlessly without control (WCAG 2.2.2);
  - **D-03:** media and video edits revalidate every page that embeds them (cache tags);
  - per-request memoisation of auth and the CMS client in draft preview (27 → 1 auth lookup);
  - the cache wrapper must not silently stop caching on errors.
- **Decisions to apply once the owner and CTO decide:** Q1 (gold contrast token), Q2 (dynamic
  rendering budget).
