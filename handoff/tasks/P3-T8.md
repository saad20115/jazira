## Scope
1. `/[locale]/projects` — category filter (from the `category` options) and status filter as in
   the design, URL-driven (`?category=hvac`) and validated with zod (I1); pagination or "load more"
   as designed; Impact in numbers from `projects-page` global; Portfolio section as designed.
2. `/[locale]/projects/[slug]` — published projects only (drafts only in draft mode); hero,
   information block (client, location, year, status, scope chips), `body` via the Lexical → JSX
   converter (I2, no `dangerouslySetInnerHTML`), key deliverables, gallery, video (upload or
   `videoUrl` embed via `youtube-nocookie.com`, sandboxed iframe, I4), related projects (same
   category, excluding self).
3. **Lightbox** per "CTO decisions" §4 (keyboard, swipe, Esc, focus trap, counter).
4. `generateStaticParams` for published slugs per locale; 404 for unknown/draft slugs;
   revalidated by the P2-T1 hooks.
