### Findings
- **R-1 [major, Figma] The Core Services carousel is offset at every width.**
  - `page.module.css:325` `.carouselOuter` has no inline-start gutter. In en it starts at x=0; in ar
    it is clipped at 140.
  - Figma starts it at the container gutter and lets it bleed at inline-end.
  - **Fix:** `padding-inline-start` = the container gutter (140 / 24 / 16), bleeding at inline-end,
    so RTL mirrors through logical properties.
  - Your own `side-by-side-03` shows this, yet criterion 1 was ticked.
- **R-2 [major, CMS] The headline splits depend on a newline the admin cannot keep.**
  - The hero and CTA display/accent split relies on `\n` in `text` fields (`HomePage.ts:281`,
    `splitDisplayLines` at `page.tsx:93`) and a word-count heuristic.
  - After an editor saves, the h1 renders as "[Building the][Future with Smart Solutions]".
  - **Fix:** separate localized fields (e.g. `title` + `titleAccent`), with a new migration, a
    backfill and an updated `docs/02-content-model.md` row (the CTO approves this model change). No
    heuristics.
- **R-3 [major, perf] Mobile `/ar` CLS is 0.12–0.13 on first load** (412 px, 4× CPU, slow 4G, no
  cache).
  - Swapping in Noto Kufi Arabic grows the h1 from 140 to 197 px.
  - `fonts.ts:17,26` set `preload: false`. Preload the active locale's font and/or set fallback
    metrics (`adjustFontFallback` / size-adjust).
  - The WhatsApp `pulseRing` animates `inline-size`/`block-size`. Use transform.
  - **Run the CLS evidence throttled with a cold cache.**
- **R-4 [major] The intro mounts after the page has painted:** the hero paints at 428 ms and the
  splash appears at 454 ms, and your own webm shows the hero first.
  - SSR the overlay and decide before first paint, e.g. a nonce'd inline script reading
    sessionStorage that sets a `data-intro` attribute on `<html>`, with CSS hiding the overlay when
    it isn't needed.
  - Keep the h1 in the HTML.
