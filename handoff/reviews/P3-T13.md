   `DOMContentLoaded` fallback.**

   `aljazira_intro_seen` only there.

   - content never hidden;
   - no viewport, UA or env branches.
5. Fix the R-40 comments, and keep R-39 out of your scripts. **Don't report numbers.** The CTO's
