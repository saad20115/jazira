  - **How the 100 MB per-collection limit on `videos` is enforced while keeping 10 MB globally for images (`media`)**:
    1. **Global config (`src/payload.config.ts`)**: `upload.limits.fileSize` remains `MAX_MEDIA_FILE_SIZE_BYTES` (`10 * 1024 * 1024`, 10 MB), so `media` (and any other image upload collection) is capped at 10 MB in Payload's Busboy multipart parser (`payload/dist/uploads/fetchAPI-multipart/index.js`).
    2. **Scoped `/api/videos` multipart endpoints (`src/collections/Videos.ts` — `createVideoUploadEndpoint`)**: Wraps Payload's built-in `POST /`, `PATCH /`, and `PATCH /:id` endpoints on `videos` with `Object.create(req.payload)` overriding `config.upload.limits.fileSize` to `MAX_VIDEO_FILE_SIZE_BYTES` (`100 * 1024 * 1024`, 100 MB) and `requestSizeLimit` to `120 * 1024 * 1024` (hard stream cap). In `next.config.ts`, set `experimental.proxyClientMaxBodySize: '110mb'` so Next.js 16's `src/proxy.ts` request body buffer does not truncate `/api/videos` uploads at 10 MB.
    3. **Server-side magic-byte & size validation (`src/collections/Videos.ts` → `validateAndSanitizeVideoFile` in `src/security/uploadValidation.ts`)**: Runs in `beforeOperation` on `create` and `update`, enforcing `file.size > 0 && file.size <= MAX_VIDEO_FILE_SIZE_BYTES` (100 MB), inspecting the file's magic bytes via `detectVideoMimeFromMagicBytes()` (ISO BMFF `ftyp` header at bytes `4..7` with MP4 major/compatible brand `isom`/`iso2`/`mp41`/`mp42`/`avc1`/`dash`/`M4V ` for `video/mp4`, or EBML header `1A 45 DF A3` with `webm` DocType for `video/webm`), rejecting disguised files (e.g. HTML renamed to `.mp4`), verifying extension ↔ magic-byte consistency, and rewriting `file.name` to `<timestamp>-<16hex>.<mp4|webm>`.
