## 7. Questions for CTO

1. **`R-31` + `R-32` Single `<head>` `rAF` vs. Chromium `first-paint` (`fpMs`) under Next.js Dynamic Streaming (`M-171` #1 & #2):**
   - `LocaleRootLayout` (`src/app/(frontend)/[locale]/layout.tsx:360`) calls `await headers()` to attach the per-request CSP nonce (`docs/03-security.md` H3), while `LocaleHomePage` (`src/app/(frontend)/[locale]/page.tsx:330`) awaits 6 parallel Payload Local API queries (`getSiteSettings`, `getHomePage`, `getServices`, `getStats`, `getProjects`, `getBrands`) and emits the home page's CSS chunks (`page.module.css`, `ServicesCarousel.module.css`, etc.) in the streamed `<main>` payload.
   - Because `<head>` is flushed in the initial chunk, when `INTRO_PREPAINT_INLINE_SCRIPT` (`src/components/IntroSplash.tsx:35`) registers a single `requestAnimationFrame` in `<head>` without hiding the page (`R-26`), Chromium fires that first `rAF` callback as soon as the layout's initial stylesheet finishes loading (`decisionMs = 108..212ms` unthrottled, `311..532ms` mid-tier, `917..1168ms` throttled), whereas Chromium's `first-paint` (`fpMs`) is held until the streamed `<main>` chunk and its stylesheets finish downloading (`fpMs = 304..436ms` unthrottled, `612..1324ms` mid-tier, `1196..3036ms` throttled).
   - We also tested deferring the single `requestAnimationFrame` registration until `DOMContentLoaded` (`commit cc29892`, logged in `dod-timing.log`), which reduced the `decisionMs`–`fpMs` gap in most runs (`0–1` frames) but violated `R-31` on `412x823 mid-tier en` where `fpMs` (`656ms`) fired *before* `DOMContentLoaded` (`889ms`), painting 1 frame of the page before the overlay appeared.
