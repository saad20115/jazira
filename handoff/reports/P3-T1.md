- **`H3` & `H3a` Content Security Policy**: `buildPublicCspHeader` (`src/security/csp.ts:47`) never emits `'unsafe-eval'` or `'unsafe-inline'` in any environment (`NODE_ENV` does not relax public CSP). `H3a` (`'wasm-unsafe-eval'` and `worker-src 'self' blob:`) is emitted strictly when `isHomePagePath` (`src/security/csp.ts:20`) matches `/ar` or `/en`. Admin CSP relaxations are documented in `ADMIN_CSP_RELAXATIONS` (`src/security/csp.ts:81`) and scoped strictly to `/admin` and `/admin/:path*` by `buildAdminCspHeader` (`src/security/csp.ts:120`).
