  - Added `beforeValidate` hook that prefers the English source field, falls back to transliterated Arabic, preserves manual editor-supplied slugs *(Correction per R-6: in Round 1 manual slugs were trimmed rather than passed through `slugify`; in Round 2 (`f87e847`) manual slugs are normalized via `slugify` and lock `slugLocked`)*, and resolves uniqueness collisions against `req.payload.find` (`baseSlug`, `baseSlug-2`, `baseSlug-3`, …).

  - Unit-tested 10 real English titles from `design/copy/02-services.md` & `design/copy/04-projects.md`, 5 real Arabic titles with diacritics and Eastern Arabic digits, English preference, Arabic fallback, manual slug preservation, uniqueness collision resolution (`-2`, `-3`), and pattern validation.
