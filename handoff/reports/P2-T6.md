1. **`R-13` [major, security — `Users.access.unlock: isAdmin`, tests, and full audit of all Payload `users` auth endpoints]**:
   - **Fix**: Set `unlock: isAdmin` on `Users.access` (`src/collections/Users.ts:261`), preventing editors and anonymous callers from calling `POST /api/users/unlock` to bypass the `A1` account lockout (`maxLoginAttempts: 5`, `lockTime: 15 * 60 * 1000` at `src/collections/Users.ts:268`–`src/collections/Users.ts:269`) or probe whether an email exists.
   - **Self-unlock decision**: An editor CANNOT unlock themself (`Users.access.unlock: isAdmin` returns `false` for all non-admin callers). Because `executeAccess({ req }, collectionConfig.access.unlock)` runs before `db.findOne` in Payload's `unlockOperation`, a locked-out user cannot authenticate to call `POST /api/users/unlock` anyway, and restricting `unlock` strictly to `isAdmin` guarantees that a compromised or malicious editor session can neither unlock another locked account nor act as an email oracle.
