### Security Notes

- **`Z4` (`quote-requests` never creatable via REST)**: Enforced at both collection level (`create: () => false` at `src/collections/QuoteRequests.ts:355`) and field level (`create: () => false` on `readOnlyFieldAccess` at `src/collections/QuoteRequests.ts:37` and `workflowFieldAccess` at `src/collections/QuoteRequests.ts:43`).
- **`Z1` (Principle of Least Privilege & Field-Level Immutability)**: Anonymous callers receive `403` on all endpoints; editors and admins can read and update only `status`, `assignedTo`, and `internalNotes`; customer and system fields are immutable via REST/admin even for `admin`; only `admin` can delete (`src/collections/QuoteRequests.ts:357`–`src/collections/QuoteRequests.ts:359`).
- **`D2` & `S4` (No Raw IP Stored; Server-Only Salt)**: `src/lib/ipHash.ts` imports `'server-only'`, enforces a runtime browser-bundle guard (`src/lib/ipHash.ts:17`), and is protected by the ESLint `no-restricted-imports` rule in `src/components/**` and `src/app/(frontend)/**`; `QuoteRequests` validates `ipHash` against `/^[a-f0-9]{64}$/` (`src/lib/ipHash.ts:21`) so raw IP addresses can never be stored.
- **`I1` (Defense-in-Depth Validation)**: `QuoteRequests` validates `name`, `email`, `phone` (KSA & international format), `consent === true`, enum memberships (`locale`, `status`, `emailStatus`), and foreign-key existence for `service` (`services`) and `assignedTo` (`users`), and truncates `userAgent` to 300 chars (`src/collections/QuoteRequests.ts:192`) and `emailLastError` to 500 chars (`src/collections/QuoteRequests.ts:211`).
