## 1. What Changed (Round 2 — `R-42`..`R-48`)

### `R-42` [major] — Fail-safe reveal (`src/components/motion/motion.ts`, `src/components/motion/motion-controller.ts`, `src/p3-t14-motion.test.ts`, `tests/e2e/p3-t14-motion.e2e.spec.ts`)
- Wrapped controller initialization, observer callbacks (`handleEntries`), `MutationObserver` callbacks, `resume()`, `runCountUp` frame steps, and viewport fail-safe checks in `try / catch` blocks that invoke `failSafeDisable()` on any exception.
- `failSafeDisable()` reveals all reveal and count-up targets, disposes observers and timers, and removes `data-motion` from `<html>` so all content is immediately visible if the controller ever throws.
- Added a viewport fail-safe check (`checkViewportFailSafe`) driven by scroll/resize/visibility listeners and a scheduled timer (`VIEWPORT_FAILSAFE_MS` in `src/components/motion/motion.ts`) so any unrevealed target that remains in the viewport for the fail-safe window without reaching the `IntersectionObserver` threshold is revealed automatically.
- Handled background tabs (`document.visibilityState === 'hidden'`) by completing in-viewport reveals (`data-revealed="instant"`) and count-ups (`finishCountUp`) immediately when hidden or on `visibilitychange`.
- Added unit tests in `src/p3-t14-motion.test.ts` covering exception recovery, the viewport fail-safe timer, and background-tab immediate reveal.
- Added Playwright e2e coverage in `tests/e2e/p3-t14-motion.e2e.spec.ts` at desktop and mobile viewports for:
  1. Anchor jump (`/en#cta`)
  2. Fast scroll to the bottom
  3. Reload mid-page
  4. Load in a background tab (`visibilityState === 'hidden'`)

### `R-43` [major] — Touch device rest states (`src/components/ServiceCard.module.css`, `src/components/ProjectCard.module.css`, `tests/e2e/p3-t14-motion.e2e.spec.ts`)
- Added `@media (hover: none), (pointer: coarse)` rules in `src/components/ServiceCard.module.css` so `.title` sits at `translateY(0)` and `.description` is visible at rest on touch devices instead of remaining hidden at `opacity: 0`.
- Added `@media (hover: none), (pointer: coarse)` rules in `src/components/ProjectCard.module.css` so `.infoBlock` sits at `translateY(0)` and `.ctaRow` ("View project") is visible at rest on touch devices.
- Added a touch-enabled mobile e2e test in `tests/e2e/p3-t14-motion.e2e.spec.ts` verifying both `ServiceCard` `.description` and `ProjectCard` `.ctaRow` at rest.

### `R-44` [major] & `R-45` [minor] — Hero CSS entrance & background settle intro pause (`src/app/(frontend)/[locale]/page.tsx`, `src/app/(frontend)/[locale]/page.module.css`, `src/p3-t4-home.test.ts`, `tests/e2e/p3-t14-motion.e2e.spec.ts`)
- Replaced `revealAttributes(...)` on the hero eyebrow, `h1`, subtitle, and CTA group in `src/app/(frontend)/[locale]/page.tsx` with pure-CSS first-paint animations in `src/app/(frontend)/[locale]/page.module.css` (`heroEnterFadeRise` for `.heroEyebrowEnter`, `.heroSubtitle`, and `.heroCtaGroup`; `heroEnterRiseOnly` — translate only, never `opacity: 0` — for `.heroTitle`) under `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference)` with `animation-fill-mode: backwards`.
- Paused both the hero entrance animations (`R-44`) and `.heroBgMedia` (`heroBgSettle`, `R-45`) while `:global(html[data-intro='active'])` (`animation-play-state: paused`) so they do not spend their timeline behind the intro splash and play as soon as the intro ends.
- Verified in `tests/e2e/p3-t14-motion.e2e.spec.ts` that the hero entrance and background settle pause under `data-intro="active"`, run when `data-intro` switches to `seen`, keep the `h1` at full opacity throughout, end visible with JS disabled, and are disabled under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`.
